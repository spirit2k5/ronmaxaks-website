Site structure

How this website is organised.

The main pages separate services, field proof, company information and contact actions so visitors can move directly to the information they need.

Use Services when you want to understand the systems RONMAXAKS supports. Projects and Gallery contain real installation and field-work media. About explains the company background and experience, while Contact contains the published phone, WhatsApp, email, location and quote-request options.

This sitemap is also a crawl hub for search engines, helping every important public page remain connected through ordinary internal links.

Using the site

Choose the page that matches what you need.

If you are comparing security options, start with Services. If you want to see evidence of previous field work, open Projects or Gallery. About gives more company background, and Contact is the direct route for a quote, WhatsApp message, phone call or email.
